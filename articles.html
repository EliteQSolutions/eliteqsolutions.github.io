Research Notes

Evidence first. Conclusions second.

Short notes on how to research business problems, qualify opportunities, preserve source traceability, and use AI without allowing automation to outrun the evidence.

Opportunity Qualification

Five signals are not the same as one verified need.

A job posting, renewal date, new finance hire, vendor sprawl, and a cost-cutting initiative may justify research. They do not automatically prove budget, authority, timing, or provider fit.

SaaS / Vendor Spend

Renewal clustering is a question—not a savings claim.

Multiple renewals in a short period can justify a structured review. The evidence still has to establish scope, ownership, current pricing, duplication, and whether change is practical.

Source Traceability

A buyer should be able to retrace the finding.

High-confidence research is easier to trust when a receiving professional can independently open the source, understand the date, and see what the record does—and does not—support.

AI Research Operations

Use AI to compress work, not manufacture certainty.

Automation is useful for organizing, comparing, routing, summarizing, and testing. Material external claims should still have a human-verifiable evidence path and visible uncertainty when the record is incomplete.

Buyer Trust

Scope is part of the product.

A clear statement of what is included, excluded, sourced, delivered, and still unknown often builds more trust than a broad claim of expertise.

Research note · publishing queue
Public-Record Research

Contradictions are research issues, not accusations.

When two dated public records conflict, the responsible output is to show both sources and the unresolved question—not infer intent, fraud, ownership, or legal meaning beyond the record.
