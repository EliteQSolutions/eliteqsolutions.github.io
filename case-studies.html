Illustrative Sample 01 · Business Research & Opportunity Qualification

SaaS & vendor-spend friction review

Illustrative scenario: A growing professional-services company wants to know whether fragmented software purchasing and renewals are creating enough operating friction to justify a specialized procurement or spend-management conversation.

Research question

Is there evidence of material SaaS/vendor complexity, duplicated functionality, renewal exposure, or manual purchasing friction—and is there enough verified need to justify a provider introduction?

Authorized source set

Illustrative vendor list supplied by the buyer, public pricing pages, public job postings, public company information, and the buyer's stated approval/renewal workflow. No private account access.

Evidence signalWhat it supportsWhat it does not prove
Multiple tools appear to serve overlapping workflow categories.A duplication-review question is reasonable.That any tool should be cancelled.
Several renewals fall within the same 90-day period.Timing may justify a structured renewal review.Guaranteed savings or renegotiation leverage.
Purchase approvals move through email and spreadsheets.There may be measurable process friction.That a specific platform is the correct solution.
Finance and department owners describe inconsistent ownership.Decision authority and workflow should be clarified.That budget or executive sponsorship exists.

Qualification output

Need: supported enough for further discussion.
Authority: not yet verified.
Timing: potentially relevant due to renewal clustering.
Budget: unknown.
Provider fit: not concluded.

Recommended next questions

Who owns renewal decisions? Which vendor categories matter most? What is the annual spend in scope? What has already been consolidated? Is the buyer willing to evaluate a specialized provider if the evidence supports it?

Buyer receives: a source/assumption table, operating-friction summary, qualification status, unanswered questions, and a clear statement of whether an introduction is premature or reasonable.

Illustrative Sample 02 · SFIR / Public-Record Research

Source-cited chronology & entity research brief

Illustrative scenario: A professional team needs a concise chronology of publicly available business records and authorized client-provided materials before deciding what should be reviewed by retained counsel or another licensed specialist.

Research question

What public records exist, how do dates/entities connect, and where do verifiable inconsistencies or information gaps appear?

Boundaries

Public records and authorized client-provided materials only. No surveillance, private-database access, impersonation, account access, legal conclusions, or investigative authority.

Date / itemSource typeIllustrative factual note
2024-03-12 · Entity filingSecretary of State recordEntity A was formed on this date; registered-agent data recorded exactly as filed.
2024-06-08 · Public company pageArchived/public web pageA biography references Entity A but uses an earlier operating date than the filing date.
2025-01-17 · Property-related public recordCounty/public indexA record contains a matching entity name; identity linkage requires further verification.
Open questionResearch limitationThe available public record does not establish beneficial ownership or intent.

What gets highlighted

Retrievable sources, chronology, entity-name variations, filing dates, publicly visible relationships, contradictions in dated statements, and unanswered questions.

What stays out

Legal conclusions, guilt/intent claims, private-person speculation, unsupported ownership assertions, regulated forensic opinions, or instructions to evade legal process.

Buyer receives: a source index, dated chronology, entity/reference map, clearly separated facts vs. unknowns, and a limitations section that a receiving professional can independently review.

Scope & Expectations

What an Elite Q engagement should feel like

Before work

Define the question, source boundaries, intended use, deliverable, timing, exclusions, and price in writing. Sensitive information is minimized at intake.

During work

Facts, assumptions, client statements, and unknowns are kept separate. Material findings are linked to retrievable sources or marked with an explicit evidence limitation.

At delivery

You receive a written research brief designed for review—not a black-box conclusion. Scope limits and open questions remain visible.

After delivery

If the evidence supports a next step, Elite Q can help define further research or qualify a specialized provider. No referral is forced merely because one exists.
