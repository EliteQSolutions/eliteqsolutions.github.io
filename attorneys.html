Strategic Financial Intelligence Review (SFIR)

SFIR is a defined-scope source-cited research service. The exact research question, source boundaries, intended use, turnaround, deliverable, exclusions, and price are confirmed in writing before work begins.

Source index

A list of retrievable public sources and authorized client-provided materials used in the brief.

Dated chronology

A timeline of relevant filings, records, public statements, or events within the agreed research scope.

Entity / reference map

Names, entities, addresses, dates, and relationships are organized exactly as the source record supports.

Contradictions & gaps

Conflicting dates or statements are flagged as research issues, not converted into accusations or legal conclusions.

Facts vs. unknowns

Verified facts, client-provided statements, assumptions, and unresolved questions remain visibly separated.

Limitations section

The brief identifies source gaps, ambiguity, and claims the available record does not establish.

What the service does not do

No private access

No password use, account access, pretexting, unlawful data acquisition, or private-database access outside an explicitly authorized lawful source.

No surveillance

No physical surveillance, tracking, subject contact, impersonation, covert interaction, or attempts to obtain non-public information from a person.

No legal / forensic conclusion

Elite Q does not decide fraud, ownership, liability, credibility, legal relevance, admissibility, or intent.

No fabricated certainty

If a record does not establish a point, the deliverable says so. Missing evidence stays missing.

Engagement process

1. Define

Share the research question and only the minimum information necessary for an initial fit review.

2. Scope

Elite Q confirms sources, exclusions, timing, deliverable, handling expectations, and price in writing.

3. Research

Work stays inside the agreed public / authorized source boundaries and records evidence limitations as they arise.

4. Deliver

You receive a written, source-cited research brief for independent professional review.

Start with the research question.

Do not send privileged, highly sensitive, or account-access information in the first message. Describe the decision or record question at a high level and Elite Q will determine whether it fits a defined research scope.
