Attorney Research Briefing

Source-cited research without pretending to be the licensed professional.

Elite Q Solutions LLC provides defined-scope public-record research and evidence organization for professional review. The service is designed to reduce research friction and preserve source traceability—not to replace counsel, licensed investigators, forensic accountants, discovery, or other regulated work.

Scope principle: public records and authorized client-provided materials only. No surveillance, private-account access, impersonation, covert contact, legal conclusions, or regulated forensic opinions.

What an SFIR can organize

  • Relevant public entity filings, dates, names, and status changes.
  • Chronologies built from retrievable public records and authorized materials.
  • Source-cited entity/reference maps that distinguish verified links from unresolved similarity.
  • Public statements or dated records that appear inconsistent and merit professional review.
  • A clear list of evidence gaps, ambiguity, and questions the available record does not answer.

What the deliverable looks like

A written research brief with a source index, dated chronology, organized findings, facts-vs-unknowns separation, and an explicit limitations section. Illustrative structure is available on the Examples page.

What Elite Q does not decide

Elite Q does not determine fraud, beneficial ownership, credibility, liability, admissibility, intent, asset ownership, legal relevance, or litigation strategy. A public record may raise a research question without proving the legal meaning of that question.

How scope is set

Before work begins, the research question, sources, exclusions, deliverable, handling expectations, timing, and price are confirmed in writing. Do not send passwords, financial-account credentials, Social Security numbers, or other unnecessary secrets during initial intake.

When another specialist is a better fit

If the work requires licensed investigation, forensic accounting, legal advice, discovery, subpoena power, surveillance, private databases, cybersecurity testing, or another regulated capability, Elite Q should not pretend that research alone substitutes for that specialist.

Start with the question, not the conclusion.

Describe the record or decision you are trying to understand at a high level. Elite Q will first determine whether it fits a defined research scope.
