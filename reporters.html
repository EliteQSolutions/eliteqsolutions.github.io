Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of claims can you verify?
Any factual assertion that can be checked against public records — corporate filings, court dockets, property records, domain registrations, regulatory filings, and open financial data. We cannot verify sealed records, classified information, or private communications.
How is this different from a background check?
A Claim Verification Brief is claim-specific. You submit one assertion and we verify it against public evidence. We do not produce general profiles or personal dossiers.
What if the claim can't be verified?
If we determine during scope review that the claim is unverifiable through public sources, you receive a full refund within 48 hours. If research has already begun and partial findings exist, those are delivered.
Is my identity protected?
Yes. We do not disclose client identities, the subject of inquiries, or research findings to any third party. All data is stored encrypted and deleted 90 days after delivery unless you request earlier deletion.
Can I use the brief in court?
The brief is a research product, not a legal document. It presents public-record findings and analytical inferences. Admissibility depends on your jurisdiction and use case. We recommend consulting legal counsel for evidentiary questions.
What payment methods do you accept?
Credit and debit cards via Stripe. Payment is collected after scope confirmation, before research begins.
