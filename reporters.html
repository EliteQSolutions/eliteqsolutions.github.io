Source Verification

Verify the record before you publish the claim.

Elite Q Solutions can organize public records and authorized materials around a clearly defined factual question. The output is a source-cited research brief that separates verified records, client-provided statements, assumptions, conflicting evidence, and unknowns.

Request a Scope Review View Illustrative Samples
Boundary: this is research support, not editorial approval, legal advice, licensed investigation, private-database access, surveillance, or a guarantee that a claim is safe to publish.

Claim / question definition

Start with one factual question precise enough to research and falsify.

Public-record research

Use retrievable public records and authorized client-provided materials within the agreed source boundary.

Evidence separation

Verified records, conflicting records, assumptions, client statements, and unknowns remain visibly distinct.

Source index

Material findings point back to retrievable sources so an editor, reporter, counsel, or researcher can independently review them.

What the brief can answer

Examples include whether a public filing exists, whether two dated public statements conflict, whether an entity record contains a named officer or registered agent, whether a public page changed over time, or whether a public claim is supported by the currently available record.

What it does not establish

Intent, guilt, beneficial ownership, credibility, legal liability, admissibility, editorial sufficiency, or the absence of additional non-public facts. A source-cited contradiction is still a research finding—not an accusation.

Engagement process

1. Define

Describe the specific claim or record question at a high level.

2. Scope

Sources, exclusions, deliverable, timing, handling expectations, and price are confirmed in writing before work begins.

3. Research

Elite Q works within the agreed public / authorized source boundary and records limitations as they arise.

4. Deliver

You receive a written brief designed for independent editorial or professional review.
